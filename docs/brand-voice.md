# Brand voice: the thoughtful local landscaper

This is the single reference for how the website sounds. Read it before writing or editing any customer-facing words, including copy generated by AI tools.

> **In one sentence:** a friendly, thoughtful South Florida landscaper who loves plants, enjoys helping people, and takes pride in giving every outdoor space personal attention.

**Brand promise:** thoughtful landscaping, personal attention, and a genuine love for South Florida's natural beauty.

**Tagline (unchanged):** *Thoughtful landscaping, personally designed.* "Inspired by nature. Made personal." was considered and kept only as an optional secondary line; the established tagline stays.

## Who's talking

Robert. He's originally from Wisconsin, moved to South Florida years ago, and has worked in hospitality, sprinkler installation and lawn care. He fell for the sunshine and beaches first, then for the plants: the Everglades, native plants, and the tropical gardens in local neighborhoods. Landscaping is something he genuinely enjoys, not just a job.

Picture him walking through a homeowner's yard: he notices a plant, says why he likes it, asks what they'd love the space to become, and suggests possibilities without any pressure. He isn't trying to sound like a luxury landscape architect or compete with a big landscaping company. He's a down-to-earth Midwesterner who loves sharing what makes South Florida special.

**The test:** would Robert say this, out loud, to a neighbor over a cup of coffee? If not, rewrite it.

## Five voice principles

| Principle | What it sounds like | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm & neighborly** | "I'd love to hear what you have in mind." Talks to people, not "prospects." | Sales pressure, slang, over-familiarity |
| **Quietly knowledgeable** | Explains *why* a plant works in plain words: "Coontie handles shade and dry spells once it's settled in." | Jargon, showing off, claiming to know everything |
| **Personally passionate** | Specific observations: the Everglades, a native plant in a neighbor's yard, layers of tropical color | Saying "passionate" over and over; forced excitement |
| **Thoughtful & detail-oriented** | Listening first, thinking about sun, drainage and how people use the space | Promising volume or speed |
| **Humble but confident** | Comfortable naming real experience; reasonable commitments | Grand promises, inflated credentials, false modesty |

**Tone mix (a guide, not a formula):** about 40% warm and personal, 25% knowledgeable and helpful, 20% creative and inspiring, 15% professional and reassuring.

## First person ("I") vs. business voice

The business is one person, so never imply a team.

| Use "I" (Robert speaking) | Use neutral or business voice |
|---|---|
| Robert's introduction and story | Plant facts and growing conditions |
| Invitations and CTAs ("Tell me about your project") | Form labels, hints and error messages |
| Service descriptions ("I start by walking the property with you") | Privacy notice and legal text ("we" for the business is conventional here) |
| Contact page and "what happens next" | Navigation, page titles, meta descriptions |
| Personal plant observations ("a few I keep coming back to") | Photo captions and credits |

"We" is fine when it clearly means **Robert and the customer together** ("We walk the property together") or South Floridians in general ("our climate", "our neighborhoods"). Outside legal text, don't use "we" or "our team" to mean the business. In the footer and site name, refer to the business by name.

## Writing style

- Short to medium sentences with a natural rhythm. A longer one is fine when telling a story.
- Everyday words. If a technical term is useful, explain it in the same sentence.
- Sentence-case headlines that say something specific.
- At most one exclamation mark per page, and usually none.
- No forced humor, puns or clichés.
- Show enthusiasm through specifics rather than adjectives. Use the word "passion" no more than once per page.

**Words that fit:** personal, thoughtful, natural, welcoming, creative, comfortable, beautiful, practical, fresh, greenery, outdoor space, local, care, details, ideas, possibilities, garden, plants, South Florida.

**Never use** (an automated test, `tests/voice.test.mjs`, fails the build if any of these appear):

- industry-leading, world-class, unmatched, unparalleled, best-in-class
- revolutionize / revolutionizing, cutting-edge, game-changing
- premium end-to-end, one-stop, turnkey solutions, "solutions" in general (the source name "UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions" is the one exception)
- award-winning, #1, top-rated (unless verified and sourced)
- transform your outdoor oasis, oasis, elevate, exceed expectations
- premier, luxury, state-of-the-art, synergy

## Tone by section

| Section | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Home hero | Welcoming, concise | "Beautiful landscaping starts with understanding your space and what you'd love it to become." |
| Meet Robert | Personal storytelling, first person | "Then I started noticing the plants." |
| Services | Customer needs and possibilities | "Whether it's refreshing a garden bed, choosing the right plants or rethinking your outdoor space, I'm happy to explore the possibilities with you." |
| Plant guide | Local plant enthusiast sharing useful, sourced advice | "South Florida gives us some wonderful planting options, but choosing the right plant for the right spot makes all the difference." |
| Inspiration gallery | Creative, open-ended, honest about sources | "There's no single way to create a beautiful landscape. Some spaces call for clean, simple greenery, while others come alive with layers of tropical color." |
| Contact & form | The start of a conversation; instructions stay clear and plain | "Have something in mind for your yard? Tell me a little about it." |
| Errors & form help | Calm, specific, no blame, no apology | "Please include an email address or a phone number so I can reply." |
| Reviews | Customers' own words only | Real, permitted reviews; nothing written on their behalf |

## Signature themes

Use these as tonal touchstones, not slogans to repeat.

- **Personally inspired:** "Every yard has its own possibilities. I enjoy finding the details that make a space feel right for the people who use it."
- **Rooted in South Florida:** "One of my favorite things about living here is the incredible variety of plants and natural beauty all around us."
- **Thoughtfully created:** "Sometimes a few carefully chosen plants can make just as much difference as a bigger landscaping project."

| Before | After | Why |

## Authenticity standards

Never write or imply anything Robert hasn't confirmed:

- Years in business or years of experience (beyond "years ago" for the move)
- Licenses, certifications, insurance, or titles such as landscape architect or horticulturist
- Awards, rankings, client counts or completed projects
- Testimonials, ratings or customer stories that aren't real and permitted
- Personal details beyond what's in `content/owner.json`

Robert's biography lives in [`content/owner.json`](../content/owner.json). Don't add to it without his input. Plant facts must follow the [plant education standards](content-guidelines.md#plant-education-standards).

## Rules for AI-generated content

When Claude or any other AI tool writes or edits copy for this site:

1. Read this file first, and keep `content/owner.json` as the only source of facts about Robert.
2. Write in Robert's voice using the "I" vs. business-voice table above.
3. Don't invent biography, credentials, experience, reviews, projects, prices, timelines or statistics. If a sentence needs a fact you don't have, leave it out or ask.
4. Avoid the banned phrases, and don't fall back on generic AI phrasing ("In today's world…", "Whether you're X or Y, we've got you covered", "Look no further").
5. Keep facts the same while improving wording. Don't change the tagline, CTA labels ("Start Your Project", "Tell Me About Your Project") or service names without approval.
6. Run `npm run check`. It includes the banned-phrase test.
