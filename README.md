# Seagrape Lawn & Landscape: brand guidelines

Brand and website rules for a small landscaping startup serving Broward County, Florida.

> **"Seagrape" is a working name.** Swap in the real business name; every rule still applies.
> Prices, phone numbers and ratings in this repo are placeholders.

## 1. Brand foundation

**Positioning:** the reliable local crew that shows up on the day we said, keeps your yard healthy through wet season, and knows which plants actually survive here.

**Why "Seagrape":** a native coastal plant seen across Broward. It handles salt, heat and hurricane wind.

**Audience:** busy homeowners (30–60), new Florida transplants, seasonal residents, later small HOAs and landlords.

**Personality:** Neighborly · Dependable · Local expert · Clean & tidy

**Taglines (pick one, use it everywhere):** *Rooted in Broward.* · *Sun-tough yards, done right.* · *Your yard, on schedule.*

## 2. Voice

- Short sentences. Name days, times and prices.
- Never use "premier," "elevate," "solutions," "experience."
- Own mistakes and say how you'll fix them.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| We mow every Tuesday morning. If a storm pushes us back, we'll text you. | Elevate your outdoor living experience with our premier landscaping solutions. |
| Free quote in 24 hours. Weekly lawn care starts at $45. | Contact us today to learn more about our competitive pricing! |

**Bilingual:** Home, Services and Quote pages in Spanish at launch, reviewed by a native speaker. No auto-translate widgets. Show "Hablamos español" only if true.

## 3. Logo

- **Versions:** primary (light backgrounds), reversed (on Seagrape green or photos), mark only (favicon, avatar, hats), one-color (vinyl, embroidery).
- **Clear space:** half the leaf's height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** lockup 140px / 1.5 in; mark 24px.
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate, add shadows, place on busy foliage, recolor veins (red, green or yellow only), or swap in a palm tree.
- **Priority:** truck door, yard signs and crew shirts before the website.

> The SVG wordmarks use live text. Before sending to a printer or sign shop, open them in a design tool with the fonts installed and convert text to outlines.

| Allamanda | `#F4C542` | Quote and call buttons **only** |

| Mangrove | `#14211B` | Body text, one-color logo |

**Rough page mix:** 55% light background · 20% Seagrape · 10% Mangrove · 8% Sawgrass/Intracoastal · 5% Allamanda · 2% Poinciana.
**Contrast:** always dark Mangrove text on Allamanda yellow, never white.

## 5. Typography

- **Headlines:** Bricolage Grotesque, 700–800, sentence case.
- **Body:** Atkinson Hyperlegible, 400/700, never below 16px.
- **Scale:** H1 56 · H2 36 · H3 22 · Body 17 · Label 12 uppercase.
- Prices and phone numbers in bold.

## 6. Photography & icons

- **Shoot:** before/after from the same spot; morning or late afternoon light; crew in branded shirts; the clean truck.
- **Avoid:** stock northern lawns, fall leaves, snow; house numbers, plates or faces without permission; heavy filters.
- **Icons:** one line-icon set (Lucide or Phosphor), 2px stroke, Seagrape on light, Sawgrass on dark. No emoji.

## 7. Broward specifics

| Period | What happens |
|---|---|
| Nov–May | Dry season. Mow every other week (Nov–Mar). |
| Jun–Oct | Wet season. Weekly mowing (Apr–Oct). |
| Jun 1–Nov 30 | Hurricane season. Push palm trimming and storm prep. |
| Jun–Sep | Many cities restrict nitrogen/phosphorus fertilizer. |

- **Service area:** start with 3–5 neighboring cities (example: Plantation, Davie, Sunrise, Weston, Cooper City).
- **Credentials to show (only if held):** Local Business Tax Receipts, liability and workers' comp insurance, Florida Limited Commercial Fertilizer Applicator certification (UF/IFAS GI-BMP), ISA arborist for tree work.
- **Plants to feature:** St. Augustine 'Floratam', Zoysia, seagrape, cocoplum, firebush, coontie, muhly grass, sabal palm.
- Don't recommend "hurricane cuts" on palms.

> Fertilizer, watering and licensing rules vary by city and change over time. Confirm with Broward County, your city, SFWMD and FDACS before publishing exact dates.

## 8. Website

**One job:** get the quote request.

**Homepage order:**
1. Hero: city-specific headline, real yard photo, Free Quote button and tap-to-call number
2. Trust strip: licensed & insured, Google rating, Hablamos español
3. Services: 4–6 cards with photo, one line, "from $" price
4. Before/after slider: 3 real jobs
5. How it works: request → free walk-through → same crew every week
6. Service area
7. Reviews: 3 Google reviews with first name and city
8. Quote form: 5 fields max, with photo upload

**Mobile:** sticky bottom bar with **Call · Text · Free Quote** on every page.

**Buttons:** one yellow button per screen; button text says what happens next.

**Sitemap:** Home · Services (Lawn care, Landscape design & install, Palm & tree trimming, Sod, Mulch & rock, Irrigation checks, Hurricane prep & cleanup) · Service areas (one page per core city) · Our work · About · Reviews · Free quote · Español

**Technical must-haves:**
- Mobile-first; tap-to-call everywhere
- Pages load in under 2.5 s; images in WebP
- Name, address and phone identical on site, Google Business Profile, Nextdoor and Yelp
- LocalBusiness schema with service area
- Form leads delivered by text message
- Descriptive alt text on every photo
